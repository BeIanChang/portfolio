# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hackathon reviewers, hiring managers, and engineers skimming a candidate link. Context: 30–90 seconds on first pass, often from a phone, deciding whether this person is a serious systems/AI engineer. The job to be done: make depth legible fast — production systems, research, range — without hype.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Yangyang (Ian) Zhang — systems engineer working on AI infrastructure: a self-hosted voice-AI platform in production, Mitacs-funded LLM-serving research, and a dozen shipped products. Success = the reader concludes "this person ships real systems" and reaches out / advances the application.

## Brand Personality

Precise, load-bearing, quiet. The voice of an instrument panel: every element labeled, nothing decorative, confidence from restraint. Evokes trust and technical credibility, never excitement-by-decoration.

## Anti-references

- Gradient-washed "AI portfolio" templates; glassmorphism cards; floaty animations (the previous version of this site).
- Navy+gold, pastel pills, bright avatar blobs, rounded-everything — the owner explicitly rejects these as juvenile.
- Template hero-metric SaaS pages; identical card grids; eyebrow labels over every section.

## Design Principles

- **Ledger, not brochure.** Work is presented as a precise index — rows, hairlines, mono metadata — the way an engineer logs systems, not the way a marketer sells them.
- **Evidence over adjectives.** Every claim carries a number, an artifact, or a mechanism. No "passionate", no "innovative".
- **One accent, spent deliberately.** Mint exists to mark signal (links, live status, the hero diagram). If everything glows, nothing does.
- **Composed, not decorated.** Linear/Vercel register: the design is the typography, the spacing, and the borders. Imagery is custom technical drawing (SVG), never stock.
- **Instrument-panel motion.** One orchestrated hero reveal; everything else is instant. Content never gated on animation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 on graphite; mono meta ≥4.5:1. Full `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives. Semantic landmarks, keyboard-reachable nav, focus-visible rings in mint.
